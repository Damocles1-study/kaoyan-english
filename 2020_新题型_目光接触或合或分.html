(41) 第②段 · Monica Moore · 从婴儿到成人黄皮书备选 B C

(41) ＿＿＿＿＿＿＿＿

We know that a typical infant will instinctively gaze into its mother’s eyes, and she will look back. This mutual gaze is a major part of the attachment between mother and child. In adulthood, looking at someone else in a pleasant way can be a complimentary sign of paying attention. It can catch someone’s attention in a crowded room. “Eye contact and smiles” can signal availability and confidence, a common-sense notion supported in studies by psychologist Monica Moore.

（41）目光接触可以是一种友好的社交信号。我们知道，一个正常的婴儿会本能地凝视母亲的眼睛，母亲也会回望。这种相互凝视是母子依恋的重要组成部分。成年以后，用让人舒服的方式看着别人，可以表示你在留意对方，带着欣赏的意味。在拥挤的房间里，它能把某个人的注意力吸引过来。「目光接触加微笑」可以传递出「我愿意交往」和自信——这个常识性的看法得到了心理学家莫妮卡·摩尔多项研究的支持。
41 → C　Eye contact can be a friendly social signal.
🎯 发现句在段中和段尾：②❸ can be a complimentary sign of paying attention · ②❺ can signal availability and confidence——两句都是「目光能传递什么」，sign／signal ＝ social signal；传递的内容（关注、欣赏、愿意交往、自信）全是好的 ＝ friendly。连句式都对得上：can be ＋ 名词。
🧩 ①❷ 是铺垫：婴儿凝视母亲、母亲回望——依恋，社交关系的起点；②❸ 起切到成人。全段一条线：从母子到社交，对视一直是在「拉近」。②❹ 在人堆里吸引注意，同样是「信号」。
✂️ 原文本来写的是 friendly：looking at someone else in a friendly way——考卷改成 pleasant，加上粗体段首句被挖走，friendly 在本段零出现。命题人不想让你靠找词做这道题。

(42) ＿＿＿＿＿＿＿＿

Neuroscientist Bonnie Auyeung found that the hormone oxytocin increased the amount of eye contact from men toward the interviewer during a brief interview when the direction of their gaze was recorded. This was also found in high-functioning men with some autistic spectrum symptoms, who may tend to avoid eye contact. Specific brain regions that respond during direct gaze are being explored by other researchers, using advanced methods of brain scanning.

（42）目光接触背后的生物学因素正在被研究。神经科学家邦妮·欧阳发现，在一次简短的面谈中记录男性被试的视线方向时，激素催产素会增加他们看向面谈者的目光接触。在有一些自闭症谱系症状、往往回避目光接触的高功能男性身上，也发现了同样的效果。另有研究者正在用先进的脑扫描方法，探究直视时会作出反应的具体脑区。

(43) ＿＿＿＿＿＿＿＿

With the use of eye-tracking technology, Julia Minson of the Harvard Kennedy School of Government concluded that eye contact can signal very different kinds of messages, depending on the situation. While eye contact may be a sign of connection or trust in friendly situations, it’s more likely to be associated with dominance or intimidation in adversarial situations. “Whether you’re a politician or a parent, it might be helpful to keep in mind that trying to maintain eye contact may backfire if you’re trying to convince someone who has a different set of beliefs than you,” said Minson.

（43）目光接触可能带有攻击性。哈佛大学肯尼迪政府学院的朱莉娅·明森借助眼动追踪技术得出结论：目光接触传递的信息可以截然不同，要看情境。在友好的情境中，它也许是亲近或信任的标志；在对立的情境中，它更可能和压制或恐吓联系在一起。「不管你是政客还是家长，记住这一点也许有好处：如果你想说服一个和你观念不同的人，硬要和他保持目光接触，可能会适得其反。」明森说。
43 → G　Eye contact can be aggressive.
🎯 While 让步，重心在主句：④❷ While eye contact may be a sign of connection or trust in friendly situations, it’s more likely to be associated with dominance or intimidation in adversarial situations——从句让一步（友好时是信任），主句才是作者要说的（敌对时是压制、恐吓）。dominance · intimidation · adversarial 的公约数 ＝ aggressive。
🧩 首尾都在为「敌对」服务：④❶ 先立总论「看情境」，④❷ 分两种情境、重心落在敌对，④❸ 明森给的建议只针对「观念不同的人」——说服对手时盯着他看会适得其反。三句里只有 ④❷ 的从句讲友好，而它是让步。
🧭 引子早预告过：①❷ it can also be antagonistic … a political candidate turns toward their competitor during a debate … signals hostility——辩论台上的「对手」与 ④❷ 的 adversarial、④❸ 的 politician、different set of beliefs 是同一个场景。

(44) ＿＿＿＿＿＿＿＿

05长难句精析（11 句 · 本篇黄皮书无此栏目，全部原创）

科普报道体的句子难在挂件多：一个主干上挂时间状语、方式状语、同位语、分词、从句，主干本身很短。先找谓语，再把挂件一件件摘下来。

But it can also be antagonistic, such as when a political candidate turns toward their competitor during a debate and makes eye contact that signals hostility.
· such as when …：such as 后面直接接 when 从句，用一个场景举例——比 for example 更口语。
· when 从句里两个并列谓语 turns toward … and makes …，主语都是 a political candidate；their 指代单数的 candidate（不分性别的单数 they）。
· that signals hostility 修饰 eye contact。also 回指 ①❶ 的「友好」——这一句是全文「或分」的一半。
译：但它也可能带着敌意，比如辩论中一位政治候选人转身面向对手，投去充满敌意的目光。
“Eye contact and smiles” can signal availability and confidence, a common-sense notion supported in studies by psychologist Monica Moore.
· 主干：“Eye contact and smiles” can signal availability and confidence。
· a common-sense notion 是同位语，复指前面整个句子说的看法（不是某一个名词）；supported in studies by … 是过去分词短语作后置定语，修饰 notion。
· 句末同位语 ＋ 分词，是英语给前一句「贴评价、给出处」的常用法：先说结论，再补一句「这是常识，研究也证实了」。
译：「目光接触加微笑」可以传递出愿意交往和自信——这是一个常识性的看法，也得到了心理学家莫妮卡·摩尔多项研究的支持。
Neuroscientist Bonnie Auyeung found that the hormone oxytocin increased the amount of eye contact from men toward the interviewer during a brief interview when the direction of their gaze was recorded.
· found 的宾语从句主干只有 the hormone oxytocin increased the amount of eye contact；后面三个挂件：from … toward …（方向）· during …（时间）· when …（条件）。
· ⚠️ brief 在挂件里：它修饰的是实验里的面谈，是方法层的词——A 项 brief 的钩子就在这。
译：神经科学家邦妮·欧阳发现，在一次简短的面谈中记录男性被试的视线方向时，激素催产素会增加他们看向面谈者的目光接触。
Specific brain regions that respond during direct gaze are being explored by other researchers, using advanced methods of brain scanning.
· 主语 Specific brain regions ＋ 定语从句 that respond during direct gaze；谓语是现在进行时的被动 are being explored——和 E 项 are being investigated 同一个时体。
· using … 的逻辑主语是 other researchers（被动句的施动者），不是句子主语 brain regions——读的时候别让「脑区使用先进方法」。
译：另有研究者正在用先进的脑扫描方法，探究直视时会作出反应的具体脑区。
While eye contact may be a sign of connection or trust in friendly situations, it’s more likely to be associated with dominance or intimidation in adversarial situations.
· While … 是让步／对比从句：重心永远在主句。
· 两半各有一个「情境」状语：in friendly situations ⟷ in adversarial situations，中心词 situations 重复，形容词对立——这是在把 ④❶ 的 depending on the situation 拆成两种。
· may be ⟷ more likely to be：前一半「也许」，后一半「更可能」——连情态都偏向主句。
译：在友好的情境中，目光接触也许是亲近或信任的标志；而在对立的情境中，它更可能和压制或恐吓联系在一起。
“Whether you’re a politician or a parent, it might be helpful to keep in mind that trying to maintain eye contact may backfire if you’re trying to convince someone who has a different set of beliefs than you,” said Minson.
· 开头 Whether A or B 是让步状语「不管是 A 还是 B」；主干 it might be helpful to keep in mind that …，it 是形式主语。
· that 从句的主语是动名词短语 trying to maintain eye contact，谓语 may backfire；后面再挂 if 条件。
· different … than you：美式口语常用 than，规范写法是 different from。考场上见到别以为是比较级。
译：「不管你是政客还是家长，记住这一点也许有好处：如果你想说服一个和你观念不同的人，硬要和他保持目光接触，可能会适得其反。」明森说。
These pauses typically occur at about three per second, and the eyes then jump to another spot, until several important points in the image are registered like a series of snapshots.
· 两个并列分句 ＋ 一个 until 从句：停（occur）→ 跳（jump）→ 直到记下（registered）——一句话写完一个循环过程。
· register 在这里是「（被大脑）记下、意识到」，不是「登记、注册」（熟词僻义）。like a series of snapshots 是比喻：一张一张拍下来，不是一次看全。
译：这种停顿通常每秒大约三次，接着目光跳到另一个点，直到图像上几个重要的点像一组快照那样被记下来。
How the whole image is then assembled and perceived is still a mystery although it is the subject of current research.
· How … perceived 是主语从句，谓语 is still a mystery。
· although 从句里的 it 指整个主语从句的内容；the subject of ＝「……的对象、课题」（不是「主语、科目」）。
· ⚠️ E 项 are being investigated 的钩子就在这个 although 从句里——段尾让步的半句，不是发现。
译：至于整幅图像随后是怎样被拼合、被感知的，至今仍是个谜，尽管这正是眼下的研究课题。
In people who score high in a test of neuroticism, a personality dimension associated with self-consciousness and anxiety, eye contact triggered more activity associated with avoidance, according to the Finnish researcher Jari Hietanen and colleagues.
· 主干在中间：eye contact triggered more activity。前面是范围状语（In people who …），后面是出处（according to …）。
· a personality dimension … 是 neuroticism 的同位语——D 项 personality 的原词就藏在这个同位语里；两个 associated with 一个解释神经质、一个解释大脑活动。
译：据芬兰研究者贾里·希塔宁及其同事的研究，神经质是一个与过分在意自己、焦虑相关的人格维度，在神经质测试中得分高的人，目光接触会引发更多与回避有关的活动。
“Our findings indicate that people do not only feel different when they are the centre of attention but that their brain reactions also differ.”
· not only … but (also) … 拆在两个 that 从句上：indicate that A but that B——第二个 that 重复出现，表明 but 连接的是两个宾语从句。also 后移到从句里。
· feel different（主观感受）→ brain reactions differ（客观反应）：递进到「连大脑都不一样」，正是 D 项 reacts 的出处之一。centre 是英式拼写（美式 center），考卷照原文保留。
译：「我们的发现表明，人们在成为众人注意的焦点时，不仅感受不同，大脑的反应也不同。」
A more direct finding is that people who scored high for negative emotions like anxiety looked at others for shorter periods of time and reported more comfortable feelings when others did not look directly at them.
· 表语从句 that …：主语 people ＋ 定语从句，两个并列谓语 looked … and reported …。
· ⚠️ when … 只管第二个谓语（reported more comfortable feelings），说的是「别人不看他们时」更自在——F 项把它改成「大多数人和陌生人对视时不自在」，主语、条件都换了。
译：一个更直接的发现是：负面情绪（如焦虑）得分高的人，看别人的时间更短；别人不直视他们时，他们自述感觉更自在。

X can show that you are paying attention in a friendly way. But it can also be antagonistic.
📌 一好一坏两句起笔：开头先承认两面，后文再分说。The Internet can bring people together. But it can also drive them apart.——大作文写「双刃剑」类话题的首段骨架。
Here’s what hard science reveals about X:
📌 冒号引出清单：作文里换成 Here is what recent surveys reveal about…，后面分点给论据，阅卷人一眼看到结构。
…, a common-sense notion supported in studies by psychologist Monica Moore.

Eye contact can signal very different kinds of messages, depending on the situation.
📌 辩证收口：depending on the situation／context／how it is used——给结论留余地，避免绝对化（本篇两个多余项正是死在「绝对」上）。

📌 While 对比句：一句话把两种情况都写进去，重心放在主句。While competition may motivate some students, it is more likely to cause anxiety in others.

📌 backfire（适得其反）：写家长施压、过度管理、强行推销类话题的好词。Pushing children too hard may backfire.
People do not only feel different when they are the centre of attention but their brain reactions also differ.
📌 不仅……而且（递进到更深一层）：从感受递进到生理、从个人递进到社会。the centre of attention（众人瞩目）本身也好用。

📌 手机与面对面交流：原文作者引特克尔的话——在真正的对话里我们学会对视、学会共情。写「低头族」「线上社交」话题可用：With people so often looking down at phones, eye contact can be an even more powerful way to communicate.（原文末句）
