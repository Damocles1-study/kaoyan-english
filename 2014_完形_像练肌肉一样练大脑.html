05固定搭配 & 熟词僻义（这些是死记就得分的）

hit middle age　人到中年（①❶）· not what sb／sth used to be　大不如前
hit 的熟词僻义＝到达（某个年龄、数字、阶段）：hit 40（到四十岁）、sales hit a record high（销量创新高）。not what it used to be 是「今不如昔」最地道的说法：The town is not what it used to be.
put sth + 地点　把……放在某处（1 题）
⭐ put／place／lay／leave（留在某处）离不开地点；地点被挪到从句开头时就是 where：I can't remember where I left my phone.
refer to A as B　把 A 称作 B（①❸）
⭐ 「称作」一族都是 A as B：refer to／regard／describe／think of A as B。refer to 单用＝提到、查阅：refer to a dictionary。
While (it is) seemingly innocent, …　尽管看似无害，……（3 题）
⭐ While／Though／If ＋ 形容词 是省略从句（省了「主语＋be」，主语与主句相同）：While popular, the policy is costly. ✍️ 作文里一句话写出「表面—实质」。
have a(n) damaging／positive／profound impact on　对……有损害性／积极／深远的影响（4 题）
impact 的介词永远是 on；形容词按方向选：damaging／negative／adverse（负）· positive／beneficial（正）· profound／far-reaching（深远）· limited（有限）。
professional, social, and personal wellbeing　职业、社交与个人的幸福（5 题）
wellbeing（well-being）＝身心与生活的良好状态：mental wellbeing（心理健康）· the wellbeing of the elderly（老年人福祉）。多个修饰语并列，每个都要搭得上、彼此不包含。
It turns out that …　结果表明，原来……（6 题）
⭐ 形式主语句型：It turns out／It seems／It appears／It happens／It follows that。It 找不到先行词时，就往这里想。turn out 另有「结果是、证明是」：The rumor turned out (to be) false.
in much the same way (as) sb／sth does　与……差不多一样（②❷）· much like　很像（④❸）
比喻的开关与收口：in much the same way (that) our muscles do（do 替 need exercise）。much 修饰 the same／like，表「非常」：much the same（大致相同）。
mental workouts　脑力训练（7 题）· cognitive functions　认知功能（8 题）
workout＝一次健身训练（go for a workout）；cognitive 是脑科学高频形容词：cognitive abilities／decline／skills。
a process of doing　做某事的过程（9 题）· is essentially a …　本质上是……
下定义的标准句：Learning is essentially a process of making mistakes and correcting them.
excel in／at (doing) sth　擅长，出类拔萃（10 题）
⚠️ 同接 in 的四个动词：excel in（擅长）· persist in（坚持）· believe in（相信）· feature in（出现在、起作用）——介词相同，只能靠意思。名词 excellence、形容词 excellent 同根。
To a certain extent, … However, …　在一定程度上……；然而……（11 题）
⭐ 先部分承认，再亮观点：to a certain／some extent ＝ to some degree ＝ in part。✍️ To a certain extent, talent matters. However, it is persistence that makes the difference.
vary／change／fluctuate according to　随……而变化（12 题）
⭐ 变化动词 ＋ according to／with／depending on；反面是 regardless of（不管、不随……变）——两者常一起出现当正反选项。
take sth a step further　把……更推进一步（13 题）
⚠️ 对照 take a step back（退一步冷静看）· step aside（让位）。✍️ Some cities have taken it a step further by banning plastic bags altogether.
improve and regain one's mental sharpness　改善并恢复头脑的敏锐（14 题）
re- 动词＝回指：regain／restore／recover 都预设「先失去了」。sharp 的熟词僻义：头脑灵敏（a sharp mind）、急剧的（a sharp rise）。
allow／enable sb to do　使某人能够做（15 题）
「使某人做」族分两栏：赋能 allow／enable／help／empower ⟺ 施压 force／compel／urge；提醒 remind sb to do。
keep track of　跟踪记录，掌握……的动态（16 题）· lose track of　弄不清……
⭐ keep 家族按介词分：keep track of · keep pace with · keep hold of · keep an eye on · keep in touch with。
provide detailed feedback on　就……提供详细反馈（17 题）
⭐ 「意见类」名词 ＋ on：feedback／comment／advice／views／report on。⚠️ provide sth for sb ／ provide sb with sth——接收者与被提供物各有位置。
Most importantly, …　最重要的是（④❸）
说明书、介绍文的收尾信号：列完几个功能，用它亮出最核心的一个。同族：Above all · Crucially · Last but not least。
build on sth　以……为基础，在……的基础上发展（19 题）
⭐ 四个 ___ on 一起背：carry on（继续进行）· put on（穿上／增重）· take on（承担／呈现）· build on（在基础上发展）。✍️ Good teachers build on what students already know.
increase resistance and vary your muscle use　不断加阻力、变换肌肉用法（④❸）
resistance 的熟词僻义＝（健身）阻力、负重；vary 作及物动词＝改变、使多样化：vary your diet（饮食多样化）。

07长难句精析（正文蓝色虚线句）

As many people hit middle age, they often start to notice that their memory and mental clarity are not what they used to be.
  • 主干：they often start to notice that …；句首 As many people hit middle age 是时间状语从句（As＝当……时、随着）。
  • ⭐ 宾语从句里套了一个表语从句：their memory and mental clarity are not what they used to be——what 从句作表语，what they used to be＝「它们过去的样子」，they 指 memory and mental clarity。
  • 💡 not what sth used to be＝大不如前，一句话定了全文第一段的负方向（2 题 fades 就是它的动词版）。
  • 🔤 hit 取「到达」：hit middle age／hit forty／hit the headlines。
译：很多人到了中年，常常开始发觉自己的记忆力和头脑的清晰度大不如前。
We suddenly can't remember where we put the keys just a moment ago, or an old acquaintance's name, or the name of an old band (that) we used to love.
  • 主干：We can't remember A, or B, or C——remember 带三个并列宾语，第一个是宾语从句，后两个是名词短语。
  • ⭐ 三个宾语长短不一、形式不同，但地位平等：看见 , or 两次出现，就回头找 remember——这是完形 1 题的定位法：三件事都是「想不起来的东西」，第一件是「钥匙放哪儿」（where）。
  • 💡 句末 an old band we used to love：省略关系代词 that 的定语从句，used to love＝过去常喜爱（现在不一定）。
译：我们会突然想不起刚才把钥匙放在哪儿了，或是想不起一位老熟人的名字，或是一支曾经喜爱的老乐队的名字。
While (it is) seemingly innocent, this loss of mental focus can potentially have a damaging impact on our professional, social, and personal wellbeing.
  • 主干：this loss … can have a damaging impact on …；句首是省略的让步状语从句：While (this loss is) seemingly innocent。
  • ⭐ 省略的条件：从句主语与主句主语相同、谓语是 be ⟹ 可把「主语＋be」一起省掉，只留 While ＋ 形容词（3 题）。
  • 💡 两个副词对撞：seemingly（表面上）↔ potentially（潜在地）——一个说「看起来」，一个说「可能会」，中间就是让步—转折的缝（02+++）。
  • 🔤 句末三个形容词并列修饰 wellbeing：professional, social, and personal——三块互不包含（5 题）。
译：尽管看似无害，这种注意力的丧失却可能对我们的职业、社交和个人幸福造成损害。
It turns out that the brain needs exercise in much the same way (as) our muscles do, and the right mental workouts can significantly improve our basic cognitive functions.
  • 结构：and 连接两个分句——前一个是 It turns out that …（It 形式主语，that 从句是真主语），后一个是简单句 the right mental workouts can improve …。
  • ⚠️ and 后面那半句还在不在 that 从句里？两种读法都通；按意思，「合适的脑力训练能改善认知」也是研究发现的一部分——读成都属于 turns out 的内容更顺（黄皮书也这么切）。
  • 💡 in much the same way our muscles do：do 替代 need exercise（避免重复）；the same way 后省略了 as／that。
  • 🔤 比喻的开头：brain ↔ muscles，全文到④段末 much like … exercise routine 才收（02++）。
译：研究发现，大脑就像肌肉一样需要锻炼，合适的脑力训练能显著改善我们的基本认知功能。
To a certain extent, our ability to excel in making the connections that drive intelligence is inherited.
  • 主干：our ability … is inherited——主语中心词 ability 和谓语 is 之间隔了 9 个词。
  • ⭐ 主语的后置修饰一层套一层：ability ← to excel in making the connections（不定式定语）← that drive intelligence（定语从句修饰 connections）。拆法：先找 is，再往回数——is 前面那一大串都是主语。
  • 💡 that 从句的主语是 connections（复数），所以谓语是 drive 不是 drives——drive 取「推动」义（连接推动智力）。
  • 📌 To a certain extent 放句首给整句打折：「在一定程度上」＝只承认一部分 ⟹ 下一句 However（11 题）。
译：在一定程度上，我们善于建立那些推动智力发展的连接的能力，是遗传而来的。
However, because these connections are made through effort and practice, scientists believe that intelligence can expand and fluctuate according to mental effort.
  • 主干：scientists believe that …；句首先后挂了两样：连接副词 However ＋ 原因状语从句 because …。
  • ⭐ because 从句与 that 从句是一条因果链：连接靠努力建立（因）⟹ 智力随努力变化（果）。12 题 according to 就是这条因果链在介词上的体现。
  • 💡 expand and fluctuate 并列：expand（增长）只说往上，fluctuate（起伏）说有升有降——智力不是只增不减，不练也会掉，所以才需要「不断」训练（18 constantly）。
译：然而，由于这些连接是通过努力和练习建立起来的，科学家相信，智力能够随着脑力的投入而扩展和起伏。
Now, a new Web-based company has taken it a step further and developed the first "brain training program" designed to actually help people improve and regain their mental sharpness.
  • 主干：a company has taken it a step further and (has) developed the first program——and 连两个谓语，共用 has。
  • ⭐ it 回指上一段整套理论（大脑可练、智力可变）；take it a step further＝把理论推进到产品（13 题）。
  • 💡 designed to actually help …：过去分词短语作后置定语，修饰 program（＝which is designed to…）。help people improve and regain：help sb do，省略 to。
  • 🔤 regain＝重新获得，预设「先丢了」⟹ 回首段找 clarity（14 题）。
译：如今，一家新的网络公司更进一步，开发出了首个「大脑训练程序」，旨在切实帮助人们改善并恢复大脑的敏锐度。
Most importantly, it constantly modifies and enhances the games (that) you play to build on the strengths (that) you are developing—much like an effective exercise routine requires you to increase resistance and vary your muscle use.
  • 主干：it modifies and enhances the games——两个并列谓语共用宾语 the games。
  • ⭐ 两个省略 that 的定语从句：the games you play · the strengths you are developing——读到「名词 ＋ 主语 ＋ 动词」就知道中间省了关系代词。
  • 💡 to build on … 是目的状语（调整游戏是为了在长处上继续提升），不是 play 的宾语补足。
  • ⭐⭐ 破折号后 much like 引导类比：much like ＋ 完整句子（an effective exercise routine requires you to …），口语里 like 可作连词＝as。比喻在这里收口，18／19／20 三个空都在这一句（02++）。
译：最重要的是，它会不断调整和强化你所玩的游戏，在你正在培养的长处的基础上继续提升——这很像一套有效的锻炼计划，要求你不断增加阻力、变换对肌肉的使用方式。

08可搬进作文/翻译的表达

本篇是一篇「问题 → 依据 → 方案」的科普推介文，结构正好是大作文三段式的骨架：描述现象 → 分析原因（科学依据）→ 提出对策。话题上，「先天与后天」「坚持练习」「终身学习」「科技产品的宣传要打折看」能直接接上努力与天赋、自我提升、理性看待科技几个常考母题。

X is not what it used to be.　X 大不如前。
描述现象段的开头，比 X is worse than before 地道。Our attention span is not what it used to be in the age of short videos.（短视频时代，我们的专注力大不如前。）
While seemingly innocent, X can potentially have a damaging impact on …　尽管看似无害，X 却可能对……造成损害。
⭐ 「表面无害、实际有害」的万能句，写手机依赖、熬夜、攀比都能用。While seemingly innocent, late-night scrolling can potentially have a damaging impact on students' health and grades.
It turns out that …　研究表明／事实证明……
摆证据的开头：It turns out that regular reading significantly improves one's ability to concentrate.
X needs exercise in much the same way our muscles do.　X 就像肌肉一样需要锻炼。
⭐ 比喻论证现成句，把 X 换成 creativity／willpower／memory：Willpower needs exercise in much the same way our muscles do—the more we use it, the stronger it becomes.
To a certain extent, … is inherited. However, because …, … can expand according to effort.　在一定程度上……是天生的；然而……会随努力而增长。
⭐⭐ 「天赋与努力」话题的让步—转折骨架：To a certain extent, talent is inherited. However, because skills are built through practice, what we achieve depends largely on the effort we make.
take it a step further　更进一步
写递进、写举措：Some universities have taken it a step further and opened their libraries to local residents.
keep track of one's progress and provide feedback on …　跟踪进展并就……提供反馈
写学习方法、写建议信：A study journal helps you keep track of your progress, and a good tutor provides detailed feedback on your weaknesses.
build on the strengths you are developing　在已有长处的基础上继续提升
写个人成长、写推荐信：Instead of dwelling on weaknesses, students should build on their strengths.（与其纠缠短处，不如在长处上继续发力。）
much like an effective exercise routine requires you to increase resistance　就像有效的锻炼要求你不断加阻力
✍️ 「走出舒适区」的比喻论证：Learning, much like an effective exercise routine, requires us to keep increasing the difficulty.
⚠️ 批判性一笔：宣传说「科学证明」≠ 真有证据
