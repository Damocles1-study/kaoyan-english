作者态度（段落 · 先褒后贬）36. According to Paragraph 1, what is the author's attitude toward the AAAS's report?

[A] Critical.
[B] Appreciative.
[C] Contemptuous.
[D] Tolerant.
✅ A · 定位①❶❷（题干 Paragraph 1 ＋ the AAAS's report ⟹ 两句都在说这份报告）
正源：①❷ Regrettably, however, the report's failure to address the true nature of the crisis facing liberal education may cause more harm than good.
▸ 第一步：定方向——两句一褒一贬，however 标出重心在后一句 ⟹ 负向；Regrettably（令人遗憾地）是作者本人的评注副词，再加一票；
▸ 第二步：定强度——负向里有 critical（批评）与 contemptuous（蔑视）两档。❶ 作者主动说 deserves praise，还说出了值得表扬的具体理由 ⟹ 不是「根本看不上」⟹ 停在 critical；
▸ 第三步：反查措辞——may cause more harm than good，用的是「可能」，批评但不全盘否定 ⟹ A（⟹ 新卡 R171；老卡 R12 强度尺）。
黄皮书技巧栏（要点转述）：含褒义词不等于赞同，含贬义词不等于蔑视，褒贬相连也不等于中立——先用 Regrettably 排「完全肯定」，再用 deserves praise 排「完全否定」，最后用 however 锁定「总体批评」。
[A] Critical ✔　方向取 however 后，强度被❶ 的表扬压住。
[B] Appreciative ⚠️ 取褒的那半：← ①❶ deserves praise for affirming …。只读了让步句；❷ 句首 however 一出现，❶ 就退成铺垫。
[C] Contemptuous ⚠️ 程度升级：方向对（负向），强度错。contemptuous ＝ 鄙视、不屑一顾，与❶ 那句真心的表扬矛盾——蔑视的人不会先列出对方的优点。
[D] Tolerant ⚠️ 取褒的那半（另一种）：tolerant ＝ 「虽不赞同但能容忍、接受」。「容忍」要求原文有接受的表态（如 it is acceptable／we can live with it），本段只有表扬与批评，没有一个接受的字；而且 more harm than good 恰恰是「不能接受」。

02+专栏 · 表扬—遗憾型评论：X deserves praise for … Regrettably, however, … may cause more harm than good ——态度取 however 后，强度被表扬压在 critical；标题要装下「被评对象 ＋ 核心指控」

⭐⭐⭐ 评论一份报告、一本书、一项政策的文章，最常见的开头就是「先表扬、再遗憾」。本篇把这个动作做了三轮，每一轮都是「褒立意 → 贬诊断」。认不出轮次，36 题会被表扬句拉到 B／D，40 题会被热情句拉到 B（⟹ 新卡 R171）。

⭐ 结论三：与 2014 T1–T3 并排：T1 4／4／4／3（说辞当作者为首）· T2 4／4／3／4（角色错为首）· T3 5／4／4／2（方向反为首）· T4 3／4／4／4（清单与立场栏并列）——文章结构决定干扰项配比：本篇有两份长清单、两派标签，干扰项就从这两处造。

03长难句精析 · 15 句

本篇是《华尔街日报》评论，句子普遍长而规整，难点分五种：① 同位语与后置修饰撑长主语（s1 的 the just-released report by …、s2 的 the crisis facing …、s11 的 who … and are thus …、s12 的 once at home on campus）· ② 长清单与分号并列（s3 的 by "…" to "…"、s7 的 supports; stresses; and encourages、s8 的 calls for 四个宾语、s9 的 study of A, B and the expansion）· ③ 倒装与虚拟（s3 的 asking that it identify、s5 的 Among … are …）· ④ 冒号、破折号与引号（s10 的冒号亮论点、s12 的 "progressive," or、s13 的 —such as—）· ⑤ 对举与双关（s10 的 "The Heart of the Matter" never gets to the heart of the matter、s13 的 treat A as … while portraying B as …、s15 的 obscuring … illuminate）。每句末尾都标了「它在题里的作用」。

s1（①❶）　"The Heart of the Matter," the just-released report by the American Academy of Arts and Sciences (AAAS), deserves praise for affirming the importance of the humanities and social sciences to the prosperity and security of liberal democracy in America.
  • 主干："The Heart of the Matter" deserves praise——主谓宾。
  • 同位语：the just-released report by the AAAS 夹在两个逗号之间，说明书名是什么。
  • for doing：deserve praise for affirming … ＝ 因……而值得表扬（for 引出理由）。
  • the importance of A to B：A 对 B 的重要性——A ＝ the humanities and social sciences，B ＝ the prosperity and security of liberal democracy。
译：美国人文与科学院刚刚发布的报告《问题的核心》值得赞扬，因为它肯定了人文与社会科学对美国自由民主的繁荣与安全的重要性。
⭐ 褒的理由写得很具体——这正是 36 题「不到蔑视」的证据。
⚠️ the importance of A to B 别拆成「A 的重要性」＋「到 B」：to 连的是「对谁重要」。
⚠️ 36B Appreciative 的原料就在这一句。
📌 作文可套：The new policy deserves praise for affirming the importance of physical exercise to students' health.
s2（①❷）　Regrettably, however, the report's failure to address the true nature of the crisis facing liberal education may cause more harm than good.
  • 主干：the report's failure … may cause more harm than good——主语是名词化的 failure（「未能」）。
  • failure to do：failure to address the true nature of the crisis ＝ 未能正视危机的真正性质；address ＝ 处理、正视（熟词僻义）。
  • 现在分词后置：the crisis facing liberal education ＝ 博雅教育所面临的危机（facing 修饰 crisis）。
  • 句首两个信号：Regrettably（作者评注：令人遗憾地）＋ however（转折）。
译：然而遗憾的是，这份报告没有正视博雅教育所面临危机的真正性质，这可能弊大于利。
⭐⭐⭐ 36A 的正源：方向由 however 定、Regrettably 加票；may 说明批评而非全盘否定（R171）。
⚠️ 名词化主语：the report's failure to address X 译成「报告没有正视 X」，别译成「报告的失败」。
📌 作文可套：Regrettably, however, the plan's failure to address the real needs of students may cause more harm than good.
s3（②❶）　In 2010, leading congressional Democrats and Republicans sent letters to the AAAS asking that it identify actions that could be taken by "federal, state and local governments, universities, foundations, educators, individual benefactors and others" to "maintain national excellence in humanities and social scientific scholarship and education."
  • 主干：Democrats and Republicans sent letters to the AAAS。
  • 现在分词作伴随：asking that it identify actions——ask that 后的宾语从句用虚拟（should）identify，所以不加 -ies。
  • 定语从句：actions that could be taken by …，by 后是引号里的执行者清单（六项）。
  • 目的状语：to "maintain national excellence in …"——修饰 taken（采取行动是为了……），也是引号里的来信原话。
译：2010 年，国会民主党和共和党的领袖人物致信 AAAS，请它确定联邦、州和地方政府，大学，基金会，教育工作者，个人捐助者及其他各方可以采取哪些行动，以「保持国家在人文与社会科学学术及教育领域的卓越地位」。
⭐⭐⭐ 一句两份清单：by「谁来做」／to「为了什么」⟹ 37 题 how to 取后者 ⟹ 37D（R172）。
⚠️ 37A／37B 分别从 by 那份清单里捡了 individual、governments。
⚠️ scholarship ＝ 学术（研究），不是「奖学金」。
📌 作文可套：Parents have asked that the school identify actions that could be taken by teachers and families to reduce students' stress.
s4（②❷）　In response, the American Academy formed the Commission on the Humanities and Social Sciences.
  • 主干：the American Academy formed the Commission——主谓宾；on … 是机构名的一部分（关于……的委员会）。
  • In response：作为回应，承上（回应的是 s3 的来信）。
译：作为回应，美国人文与科学院成立了人文与社会科学委员会。
⭐ 短句承上：In response 把「来信 → 成立委员会 → 出报告」串成因果链；本句不设题。
⚠️ commission ＝ 委员会（不是「佣金」）；form ＝ 组建（不是「形式」）。
📌 作文可套：In response, the university formed a committee on student mental health.
s5（②❸）　Among the commission's 51 members are top-tier-university presidents, scholars, lawyers, judges, and business executives, as well as prominent figures from diplomacy, filmmaking, music and journalism.
  • 完全倒装：介词短语 Among the commission's 51 members 提前，谓语 are 放在主语前；真主语是后面那一长串人。
  • 主语结构：A, B, C, D, and E, as well as F——as well as 引出补充的一组。
  • top-tier-university presidents：连字符把 top-tier university 连成前置定语，修饰 presidents（一流大学的校长）。
译：委员会的 51 名成员中，有顶尖大学的校长、学者、律师、法官和企业高管，也有外交、电影、音乐和新闻界的知名人士。
⭐ 倒装的读法：看到句首 Among／Here／There ＋ be，先跳过 be 去找真主语；are 的复数就是提示。
⚠️ executive ＝ 高管（不是「执行的」）；figure ＝ 人物（不是「数字」）。
📌 作文可套：Among the volunteers are retired teachers, doctors and engineers, as well as college students.
s6（③❶）　The goals identified in the report are generally admirable.
  • 主干：The goals are admirable——主系表。
  • 过去分词后置：identified in the report 修饰 goals（报告中确定的目标）。
  • generally：总体上、大体上——留余地的副词。
译：报告所确定的目标总体上值得称道。
⭐⭐ 第二轮的「褒」：短句总领③段，generally 已经暗示「但」要来——下一段开头 Unfortunately 兑现（R171）。
⚠️ admirable ＝ 值得钦佩的、令人称道的（不是「令人惊讶的」）。
📌 作文可套：The goals of the campaign are generally admirable, but its methods deserve a closer look.
s7（③❷）　Because representative government presupposes an informed citizenry, the report supports full literacy; stresses the study of history and government, particularly American history and American government; and encourages the use of new digital technologies.
  • 原因状语从句：Because representative government presupposes an informed citizenry——代议制政府以见多识广的公民为前提。
  • 主干＝一个主语带三个谓语：the report supports …; stresses …; and encourages …——分号分隔并列谓语（因为第二项里自带逗号插入语，只好升级用分号）。
  • 插入的同位说明：particularly American history and American government，particularly ＝ 尤其是。
译：由于代议制政府以公民见多识广为前提，报告支持全民具备读写能力；强调历史与政体的学习，尤其是美国历史与美国政体；并鼓励使用新的数字技术。
⭐⭐⭐ 38C 的正源：第三个谓语 encourages the use of new digital technologies（R172 同行对齐）。
⚠️ 38A 把 particularly（尤其）升成 exclusive（只）。
⚠️ 分号为什么不是逗号：并列项内部已有逗号时，并列项之间改用分号——数分号就能数出几个并列项。
📌 作文可套：Because a healthy society presupposes informed citizens, schools should stress critical thinking.
s8（③❸）　To encourage innovation and competition, the report calls for ① increased investment in research, ② the crafting of coherent curricula that improve students' ability to solve problems and communicate effectively in the 21st century, ③ increased funding for teachers and ④ the encouragement of scholars to bring their learning to bear on the great challenges of the day.
  • 目的状语前置：To encourage innovation and competition。
  • 主干：the report calls for ①, ②, ③ and ④——calls for 带四个名词性宾语。
  • ② 的定语从句：that improve students' ability to solve problems and communicate effectively，先行词是 curricula（复数，所以 improve 不加 s）。
  • ④ 的结构：the encouragement of scholars to do（encourage sb to do 的名词化）；bring … to bear on … ＝ 把……用于、施加于。
译：为了鼓励创新与竞争，报告呼吁加大科研投入，精心设计连贯的课程以提高学生在 21 世纪解决问题和有效沟通的能力，增加对教师的资助，并鼓励学者把自己的学识用于应对当今的重大挑战。
⭐⭐⭐ 先给四个宾语编号：每个宾语的中心名词是 investment／crafting／funding／encouragement——四个名词化结构并列。
⚠️ 38B：② 的从句与 ④ 的 bring learning to bear 都指向「实用」，theoretical 方向反；38D：③ funding 的对象是 teachers，不是外语。
⚠️ curricula 是 curriculum 的复数；crafting ＝ 精心设计（craft 作动词）。
📌 作文可套：To encourage creativity, the school calls for smaller classes, more hands-on projects and increased funding for libraries.
s9（③❹）　The report also advocates greater study of foreign languages, international affairs and the expansion of study abroad programs.
  • 主干：The report advocates X and Y。
  • 并列的切分：X ＝ greater study of [foreign languages, international affairs]，Y ＝ the expansion of study abroad programs——of 管到 international affairs 为止，and 之后换了新的中心名词 expansion。
  • study abroad programs：study abroad（出国留学）整体作定语修饰 programs。
译：报告还提倡加强外语和国际事务的学习，并扩大留学项目。
⭐⭐ 38D 的另一半原料：greater study of foreign languages——外语得到的是「加强学习」，不是「资助」（R172）。
⚠️ 别切成三项并列（外语 · 国际事务 · 扩大留学）：看 the 出现的位置——the expansion 带冠词另起，前两项共用 greater study of。
📌 作文可套：The report also advocates greater study of local history and the expansion of volunteer programs.
s10（④❶）　Unfortunately, despite 2½ years in the making, "The Heart of the Matter" never gets to the heart of the matter: the illiberal nature of liberal education at our leading colleges and universities.
  • 让步状语：despite 2½ years in the making——尽管耗时两年半才完成；in the making ＝ 在制作中。
  • 主干："The Heart of the Matter" never gets to the heart of the matter——同形双关：前者带引号是书名，后者是成语 get to the heart of the matter（切中要害）。
  • 冒号后的同位成分：the illiberal nature of liberal education … 解释 the heart of the matter 到底是什么。
译：可惜的是，尽管耗时两年半才写成，《问题的核心》却始终没有触及问题的核心：我们一流高校里的博雅教育所具有的偏狭（不博雅）的本质。
⭐⭐⭐ 全文论点句 ＝ 40A 的根：冒号后 illiberal … liberal education 正是原文标题 Illiberal Education（R171／R173）。
⚠️ 40C Ways to Grasp 就是在这个双关上造的：「报告没抓住核心」被偷换成「怎样理解报告」。
⚠️ 2½ 读作 two and a half。
📌 作文可套：Unfortunately, despite months in the making, the new rule never gets to the heart of the matter: students simply have too little free time.
s11（④❷）　The commission ignores that for several decades America's colleges and universities have produced graduates who don't know the content and character of liberal education and are thus deprived of its benefits.
  • 主干：The commission ignores that …——that 引导宾语从句。
  • 宾语从句主干：colleges and universities have produced graduates，for several decades ＋ 现在完成时 ＝ 几十年来一直如此。
  • 定语从句带两个并列谓语：graduates who don't know … and are thus deprived of …——are 的主语仍是 who（毕业生），不是 colleges。
  • be deprived of：被剥夺、得不到；its ＝ liberal education's。
译：委员会忽视了这样一个事实：几十年来，美国的高校培养出的毕业生并不了解博雅教育的内容与特质，因而也无从享受它的益处。
⭐⭐ 证据一：病根的第一个症状（毕业生不懂博雅教育）；ignores 是作者给委员会定的罪名——「没诊断出」（R171 认目标、疑诊断）。
⚠️ and are thus 最容易接错主语：and 前后的谓语 don't know／are 都属于 who。
⚠️ character ＝ 特质、特性（不是「人物、字符」）。
📌 作文可套：Many schools ignore that students who never read for pleasure are deprived of its lifelong benefits.
s12（④❸）　Sadly, the spirit of inquiry once at home on campus has been replaced by the use of the humanities and social sciences as vehicles for publicizing "progressive," or left-liberal propaganda.
  • 主干：the spirit of inquiry has been replaced by the use of …——被动：by 后面的是取而代之的新东西。
  • 形容词短语后置：once at home on campus ＝ （which was）once at home on campus，曾在校园里如鱼得水、蔚然成风；at home ＝ 自在的、盛行的（熟词僻义）。
  • use A as B for doing：the use of the humanities … as vehicles for publicizing … ＝ 把人文社科当作宣传……的工具。
  • 引号＋or 改写："progressive," or left-liberal propaganda——or ＝ 也就是，作者用自己的叫法换掉对方的自称。
译：可悲的是，曾经在校园里蔚然成风的探究精神，已经被这样一种做法所取代：把人文与社会科学当作宣传「进步主义」——或者说左翼自由派——主张的工具。
⭐⭐⭐ 证据二 ＋ 排斥型引号：progressive 带引号、or 改写成 left-liberal、名词用 propaganda ⟹ 作者不认这个自称（R29／R173）。
⚠️ A is replaced by B ＝ B 取代 A，别译反。
⚠️ vehicle ＝ 工具、手段（不是「车辆」）；inquiry ＝ 探究（不是「询问」）。
📌 作文可套：Sadly, the spirit of play once at home in childhood has been replaced by the use of apps as vehicles for advertising.
s13（⑤❶）　Today, professors routinely treat the progressive interpretation of history and progressive public policy as the proper subject of study while portraying conservative or classical liberal ideas—such as free markets and self-reliance—as falling outside the boundaries of routine, and sometimes legitimate, intellectual investigation.
  • 主干：professors treat A as B——A ＝ the progressive interpretation of history and progressive public policy，B ＝ the proper subject of study。
  • while 引出对照：while portraying C as D——portray … as 与 treat … as 同构；C ＝ conservative or classical liberal ideas，D ＝ falling outside the boundaries of …。
  • 破折号插入举例：—such as free markets and self-reliance— 举的是 C 的例子。
  • 逗号插入并列修饰：routine, and sometimes legitimate, intellectual investigation——routine 与 legitimate 并列修饰 investigation，and sometimes 表递进。
译：如今，教授们习以为常地把进步主义的历史解读和进步主义的公共政策当作正当的研究课题，同时却把保守主义或古典自由主义的思想——比如自由市场和自力更生——描绘成不属于常规的、有时甚至不属于正当的学术研究范围的东西。
⭐⭐⭐ 39B 的正源：treat A as 正当 ／ portray C as 界外 ⟹ 双重标准 ⟹ biased against C（R173）。
⚠️ 39A／39D 把 C 栏的 free markets、conservative 安到教授（A 栏）头上；39C 照搬句末 intellectual investigation。
⚠️ classical liberal ＝ 古典自由主义（黄皮书译「古典人文」不妥）；routinely 是副词「惯常地」，routine 此处是形容词「常规的」。
📌 作文可套：Some parents treat test scores as the proper measure of success while portraying hobbies as a waste of time.
s14（⑥❶）　The AAAS displays great enthusiasm for liberal education.
  • 主干：The AAAS displays enthusiasm——主谓宾。
  • enthusiasm for：对……的热情（介词用 for）。
译：美国人文与科学院对博雅教育表现出了极大的热情。
⭐⭐ 第三轮的「褒」：短句独立成句，下一句 Yet 立刻反转（R171）。
⚠️ 40B Contribution 的原料：热情 ≠ 贡献。
⚠️ display ＝ 表现出（不只是「展示陈列」）。
📌 作文可套：Young people display great enthusiasm for volunteer work.
s15（⑥❷）　Yet its report may well set back reform by obscuring the depth and breadth of the challenge that Congress asked it to illuminate.
  • 主干：its report may well set back reform——may well ＝ 很可能；set back ＝ 使倒退、阻碍。
  • by doing 表方式：by obscuring the depth and breadth of the challenge——通过掩盖挑战的深度与广度（来阻碍）。
  • 定语从句：the challenge that Congress asked it to illuminate——that 在从句里作 illuminate 的宾语（Congress asked it to illuminate the challenge）。
译：然而，它的报告掩盖了国会请它阐明的那一挑战的深度与广度，很可能反而阻碍改革。
⭐⭐⭐ 首尾呼应：may well set back reform ↔ ①❷ may cause more harm than good；Congress asked ↔ ②❶ 国会来信（R171）。
⭐⭐ obscure ↔ illuminate 一暗一明的反义对：国会请它照亮，它却遮暗了。
⚠️ may well ≠ may as well：may well 很可能；may as well 不妨。
📌 作文可套：Yet the rule may well set back progress by obscuring the real problem that parents asked schools to solve.

05可搬进作文的素材 · 8 组

本篇话题——评价一项计划或报告、好目标与真问题、教育的本质、独立思考与多元观点——正对英语一大作文里「如何评价一项新举措」「教育的目的」「独立思考」「兼听则明」一类题目。
最值得搬的是四副论证动作：① 先褒后贬开头（X deserves praise for … Regrettably, however, …）· ② 书名／口号双关（X never gets to the heart of the matter）· ③ 双重标准的对举（treat A as … while portraying B as …）· ④ 首尾呼应收尾（X displays great enthusiasm for … Yet … may well set back …）。

⭐⭐⭐ X deserves praise for affirming the importance of A to B. Regrettably, however, its failure to address C may cause more harm than good.
X 值得赞扬，因为它肯定了 A 对 B 的重要性。然而遗憾的是，它没有正视 C，这可能弊大于利。
⟹ 先褒后贬的开头段（原文①❶❷）：评价任何政策、计划、现象的万能两句。
可套：The new "double reduction" policy deserves praise for affirming the importance of rest to children's growth. Regrettably, however, its failure to address parents' anxiety may cause more harm than good.
⭐⭐ The goals identified in X are generally admirable.
X 所提出的目标总体上值得称道。
⟹ 让步的总句（原文③❶）：generally 留余地，下一段就能转。
可套：The goals identified in the campus reform plan are generally admirable.
⭐⭐ Because A presupposes B, X supports C; stresses D; and encourages E.
由于 A 以 B 为前提，X 支持 C、强调 D、鼓励 E。
⟹ 用分号列举一串措施（原文③❷）：给作文加一个有条理的长句。
可套：Because lifelong learning presupposes curiosity, the school supports reading clubs; stresses hands-on projects; and encourages students to ask questions.
⭐⭐⭐ Unfortunately, despite X years in the making, Y never gets to the heart of the matter: Z.
可惜的是，尽管 Y 酝酿了 X 年，却始终没有触及问题的核心：Z。
⟹ 冒号亮出真正的问题（原文④❶）：转折段第一句。
可套：Unfortunately, despite two years in the making, the new rule never gets to the heart of the matter: students simply lack time to think.
⭐⭐ The spirit of X once at home in Y has been replaced by Z.
曾经在 Y 蔚然成风的 X 精神，已经被 Z 取代。
⟹ 今昔对比、写「变味了」（原文④❸）。
可套：Sadly, the spirit of friendly competition once at home in school sports has been replaced by the pursuit of medals at any cost.
⭐⭐⭐ People routinely treat A as B while portraying C as falling outside the boundaries of D.
人们习惯于把 A 当作 B，却把 C 说成不属于 D 的范围。
⟹ 揭示双重标准（原文⑤❶）：批评偏见、单一价值观的利器。
可套：Many parents routinely treat high scores as the proper sign of success while portraying art and sport as falling outside the boundaries of serious study.
⭐⭐⭐ X displays great enthusiasm for Y. Yet it may well set back Z by obscuring the depth and breadth of the challenge.
X 对 Y 表现出极大热情，然而它掩盖了挑战的深度与广度，很可能反而阻碍 Z。
⟹ 先褒后贬的结尾段（原文⑥❶❷），与开头首尾呼应。
可套：Society displays great enthusiasm for online education. Yet it may well set back real learning by obscuring the depth and breadth of the digital divide.
⭐⭐ X never gets to the heart of the matter.
X 始终没有触及问题的核心。
⟹ 名字与内容的反讽（原文④❶ 书名双关）：一个自称「核心」的东西偏偏没抓住核心。
可套：A campaign called "Healthy Campus" that never gets to the heart of the matter—students' sleep—is a campaign in name only.
🧭 本篇八组素材可以连成一篇「如何评价一项新举措」大作文骨架：① 先褒后贬开头（deserves praise for … Regrettably, however, … more harm than good）→ ② 让步总句（The goals … are generally admirable）→ ③ 分号列举好措施（supports …; stresses …; and encourages …）→ ④ 转折亮真问题（Unfortunately, despite … never gets to the heart of the matter: …）→ ⑤ 今昔对比（The spirit of … once at home … has been replaced by …）→ ⑥ 双重标准举证（treat A as … while portraying B as …）→ ⑦ 首尾呼应收尾（displays great enthusiasm … Yet … may well set back …）。
⭐ 这正是本文的逻辑：表扬立意 → 列出好目标 → 指出没诊断出的真问题 → 举证 → 回到开头的「弊大于利」；写进作文就是「肯定出发点，但指出它没打中要害」——比一味赞成或一味反对都更像一篇议论文。
⚠️ AAAS、国会、进步主义与保守主义这些政治标签别写进作文；要搬的是句型与论证动作，话题换成「双减」「网课」「校园手机禁令」「体测改革」即可。
